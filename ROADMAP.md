# Roadmap

What we are building next is decided in public, from requests people file.

## Asking for something

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/aabiro/xcelsior/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
It asks what you are trying to do and how you handle it today, because the
problem is what we prioritise; there may be a better way to solve it than the
feature you have in mind.

Before opening one, look through the [open requests](https://github.com/aabiro/xcelsior/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aroadmap).
If yours is there, add a thumbs-up reaction and a comment with your use case.
Comments that explain a workload, a size or a deadline move a request more than
reactions alone.

Security problems are never filed here: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What each status means

Every request carries one status label.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `status: under review` | New. We read it and reply. |
| `status: prioritized` | We intend to build it. Typically 3 to 12 months away. |
| `status: planned` | Scheduled. Typically less than 3 months away. |
| `status: in progress` | Being built now. |
| `status: released` | Shipped. The issue links the release. |
| `status: not planned` | We decided not to build it, and the issue says why. |

The timings are what we expect when we set the status, not a promise. When a
plan changes, we change the label and say why on the issue.

## Built, waiting to be switched on

Some features are finished but not on for everyone yet. The
[status page](https://xcelsior.ca/status) lists them under "Features in
preview" with their live state. To try one early, ask on its issue or through
[support](https://xcelsior.ca/support).
