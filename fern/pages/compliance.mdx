## Overview

Xcelsior is a global marketplace. Capacity comes from independent hosts around
the world, and placement is decided on price, availability, GPU model, and host
reputation. **We do not offer a data-residency guarantee**, because a
marketplace whose supply is independent operators in many countries cannot
honestly promise a workload stays in one of them.

What we do offer is enforcement you can verify: every action is scoped, every
destructive or spending action is approved by a human before it runs, and every
one of them is recorded. That is what a security review can actually check.

If a workload must be processed in a particular place, select capacity by region
at launch and use the audit trail below as evidence of where it ran.

## Access control

Credentials are scoped to the operations they need. There is no wildcard scope:
a token that can read instances cannot launch one, and a token issued for tenant
automation can never reach host or control-plane operations.

- Scopes are checked at the edge and again at the API, and the two agree.
- A scope requirement is **cumulative**. Holding one of a tool's several scopes
  is not sufficient to invoke it.
- Machine credentials — OAuth client-credentials tokens and agent API keys — are
  scope-checked identically. Narrowing a credential genuinely narrows it.

## Human approval on anything destructive or costly

Terminating an instance, deleting a volume, moving money, or committing to spend
does not execute on the strength of an argument in a tool call. The server
prepares an **action plan** describing exactly what will happen, and a human
approves that specific plan before anything runs.

- A plan is bound to the server, not the caller. Changing the request after
  approval invalidates it.
- A `confirm: true` flag is never a substitute for approval.
- Plans expire, so an unapproved plan becomes unexecutable rather than lingering.

## Audit trail

Every action is recorded with the identity that authorised it, the plan that was
approved, what ran, on which host, and what it cost. The chain is tamper-evident
and exportable.

This is evidence **after the fact**, which is the honest thing to offer. It tells
you where a workload ran. It is not a promise about where the next one will.

## Privacy

- **Consent** is recorded before a customer is charged and can be withdrawn at
  any time from Settings.
- **Retention** policies are configurable, and expired data is purged on a
  schedule rather than on request.
- **Export and erasure** are self-service from your account, and each request is
  trackable to completion.
- Personal information is handled under the privacy law applicable to Xcelsior
  Compute Inc. as a Canadian company. That is a statement about the operator,
  not a claim about where your compute runs.

## Tax

Sales tax is calculated from your billing address and appears on every invoice.
GST/HST applies to Canadian customers. It is unrelated to where a GPU sits.
